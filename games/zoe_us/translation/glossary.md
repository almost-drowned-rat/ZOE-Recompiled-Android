# ZOE subtitles from the Japanese - glossary and style

These subtitles are a new translation of the Japanese subtitles (the Japanese voices' own words), shown with the
Japanese voices at the Japanese lines' times. They follow the Japanese, not the US / EU script: where the US script
added, cut or changed something, the Japanese wins.

## Style

- Faithful and short: say what the Japanese says, no additions, no explanations. Most lines fit two lines on the
  screen (at most ~46 characters a line); a single three-line subtitle is fine where the Japanese line is long.
- Line breaks: where the Japanese breaks the line (`\n` or a full-width space between sentences), break too if it
  reads well; otherwise break at a natural pause.
- Keep the Japanese pauses and stammers: `…` becomes `...`; `―` / `ー！` shouts stay shouts.
- Numbers and codes as in the Japanese (full-width digits become plain digits: `Ｓ０２` -> `S02`).
- Button names as the PS2's: `R1`, `L2`, `START button`, `Circle` / `Cross` etc. (as the Japanese writes them).

## The game's letters

The game's font has ASCII and these accents (the disc's own `&` escapes, written by the build from the
translation's Unicode): á é í ó ú (`&'`), à è ù (`&``), â ê î ô û (`&^`), ä ö ü Ä Ö Ü ß ç Ç ë (`&~`, `&@`).
Others become their nearest (Italian ì ò -> i' o' as the Italian disc writes them). `…` becomes `...`.

## Spanish (ES) - a fifth language

Spanish has no official ZOE text: two translations of our own - from the Japanese (with the Japanese voices, like
the four others) and from the English US subtitles (with the English voices, their times). Same names as above.
